HSL Palette Generator

How to use

  1. Pick a base color : it sets the hue and saturation for the 500 shade.
  2. Adjust Hue : shift the color angle (–50° to +50°) to warm up or cool down the whole scale.
  3. Adjust Saturation : increase or decrease vividness across all shades at once.
  4. Click any swatch : to copy its hex value to your clipboard.
  5. Export : copy the SVG directly into Figma, or download it as a file.
